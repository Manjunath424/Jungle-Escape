HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls
A / D or ← / → Change Lanes (Left / Right)
W / Space or ↑ Jump over obstacles
S or ↓ Slide under overhead traps
Esc / P Pause Game
📱 Touch Gestures
Swipe Left / Right: Switch Lanes
Swipe Up: Jump • Swipe Down: Slide
⚠️ Ancient Obstacles
🪵 Logs & Spike Traps: JUMP over them
🌿 Low Branches & Stone Arches: SLIDE under them
🪨 Boulders & Pillars: DODGE by changing lanes
✨ Power-Ups
🛡️ Temple Shield: Absorbs 1 crash
🧲 Coin Magnet: Attracts all nearby gold
✨ 2X Gold: Doubles collected coin value
⚡ Score Boost: Doubles distance score
🔥 Artifact Rush: Super speed & invincible smash
